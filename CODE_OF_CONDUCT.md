# Code of Conduct

EnvPlane contributors must communicate professionally and respectfully.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are
not accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers. Do not publish
personal or sensitive information in an issue. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible.
