The software license does not grant rights to the EnvPlane name, logos, product
identity, or other brand assets.

You may make truthful statements such as "compatible with EnvPlane" or "built
using EnvPlane". You may not use EnvPlane branding in a product, service,
company, domain, or package name in a way that suggests sponsorship,
certification, or endorsement without prior written permission.

Forks and modified distributions must use distinct branding and must clearly
state that they are not official EnvPlane releases.
